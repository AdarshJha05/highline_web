Course Overview

The Basic First Aid, CPR & AED course provides essential knowledge and practical skills to respond to common workplace and everyday emergencies until professional medical assistance arrives. Participants learn basic first aid, CPR, AED use and emergency response procedures.

Who Is It For?

Suitable for students, employees, supervisors, safety professionals, workplace first aiders and anyone interested in developing essential emergency response skills.

Benefits to Learners

  • → Recognise common emergency situations
  • → Provide basic first aid
  • → Perform CPR and understand AED operation
  • → Respond appropriately to common workplace injuries
  • → Communicate effectively with emergency services
