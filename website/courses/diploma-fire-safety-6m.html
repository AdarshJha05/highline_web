Course Overview

The Diploma in Fire Safety is designed to provide learners with essential knowledge and practical skills to prevent, control, and respond to fire emergencies in the workplace. Fires can occur in any environment, including offices, factories, construction sites, and industrial plants, and proper training is necessary to minimise risks and protect lives and property. This course teaches students about the causes of fire, types of fire extinguishers, alarm systems, evacuation procedures, and emergency response techniques. With a strong focus on safety awareness and hands-on practice, learners become confident in handling fire-related incidents. This diploma is ideal for those who wish to build a career as a fire safety officer, fire marshal, or emergency response professional.

Who Is It For?

Ideal for 10th-pass students, freshers and working professionals who want to build a career in fire safety and emergency response. No prior fire safety experience is required.
