Course Overview

The PG Diploma in Fire Safety is a comprehensive professional programme designed to develop knowledge and practical skills in fire prevention, fire protection, emergency preparedness and fire risk management. The programme provides learners with an understanding of the principles of fire science, fire prevention, firefighting systems, fire safety management and emergency response.

Who Is It For?

Anyone who wants to develop professional knowledge and skills in fire safety and emergency management. Suitable for students, freshers and working professionals who want to build a career in fire safety or strengthen their existing knowledge and responsibilities related to workplace fire prevention and emergency response.

Benefits to Learners

  • → Understand the fundamental principles of fire and fire behaviour
  • → Identify common workplace fire hazards and sources of ignition
  • → Understand the different classes of fire and appropriate extinguishing methods
  • → Assess fire risks and recommend suitable control measures
  • → Understand the selection and use of firefighting equipment
  • → Develop awareness of fire detection and alarm systems
  • → Understand emergency evacuation procedures and emergency response arrangements
  • → Participate in fire safety inspections and identify areas requiring improvement
  • → Understand basic fire protection systems used in buildings and industrial facilities
  • → Support the development of workplace emergency plans and fire safety procedures

Benefits to Employers

  • → Reduce the likelihood and potential impact of workplace fires
  • → Improve fire prevention and emergency preparedness
  • → Increase employee awareness of fire hazards and safe working practices
  • → Strengthen workplace fire safety culture
  • → Improve emergency response and evacuation arrangements
  • → Protect employees, visitors, property and business operations
