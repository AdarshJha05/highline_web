OUR STORY

High Line creates a supportive space for students to master fire & safety skills and learn discipline, awareness, and leadership — in the classroom and on the drill yard.

500+Students trained 10+Certified courses 15+Industry tie-ups 4Learning paths

Starting as a local training initiative in Karimnagar, High Line has grown into an ISO 9001:2015 certified, IBSP approved, IOSH-accredited institute. With experienced trainers, modern facilities, and structured courses, we continue to help students reach their full potential.
