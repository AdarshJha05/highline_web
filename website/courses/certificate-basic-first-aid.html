Course Overview

The Basic First Aid course is a practical training program that prepares learners to provide immediate medical assistance during emergencies until professional help arrives. Accidents and sudden illnesses can happen at any time, and quick action can often save lives. This course teaches important skills such as CPR, controlling bleeding, treating burns, managing fractures, and responding to choking or fainting situations. With hands-on practice and real-life examples, learners gain the confidence to handle emergencies calmly and responsibly. First aid knowledge is valuable not only in workplaces but also in everyday life, making it an essential skill for everyone.

Who Is It For?

Suitable for students, employees, supervisors, safety professionals, workplace first aiders and anyone interested in developing essential emergency response skills.

Benefits to Learners

  • →Recognise common emergency situations quickly and calmly
  • →Perform CPR and understand AED operation
  • →Provide first aid for bleeding, burns, fractures and shock

  • →Useful for all workplaces and everyday life situations
