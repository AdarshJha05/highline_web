Our Story

Highline creates a supportive space for students to master fire & safety skills and learn discipline, awareness, and leadership — in the classroom and on the drill yard.

500+Students trained 10+Certified courses 15+Industry tie-ups 4Learning paths

Starting as a local training initiative in Karimnagar, Highline has grown into an EOSH Approved Training Provider, IOSH Approved Training Provider, and Bharat Sevak Samaj approved institute. With experienced trainers, modern facilities, and structured courses, we continue to help students reach their full potential.
