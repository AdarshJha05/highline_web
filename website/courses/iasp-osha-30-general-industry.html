IASP OSHA 30-Hour General Industry

Course Overview

The IASP OSHA 30-Hour General Industry Course has been designed to deliver various training to working professionals with health and safety responsibility. This training emphasises learners to identify, prevent, control and avert hazards in the workplace, as well as OSHA standards. Suitable for Safety Supervisors, Safety Officers, Safety Consultants, Safety Committee Members and safety professionals.

Who Is It For?

Suitable for Safety Supervisor, Safety Officer, Safety Consultancy professionals, Safety Committee Members and any Safety Professionals. Minimum 10th Pass with Good English Communication.

Benefits to Learners

  • →Increased safety awareness and reduced risk of workplace injuries
  • →Improved job performance and better employment opportunities
  • →Fewer accidents and improved OSHA compliance for employers
  • →Stronger safety culture and reduced insurance and liability costs

Course Content

01Introduction to OSHA – 2 Hours
02Managing Safety and Health – 2 Hours
03Walking and Working Surfaces including Fall Protection – 1 Hour
04Exit Routes, Emergency Action Plans, Fire Prevention – 1 Hour
05Electrical Safety – 2 Hours
06Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) – 1 Hour
07Materials Handling – 2 Hours
08Hazard Communication – 2 Hours
09Elective Topics – 10 Hours (Confined Spaces, Lockout/Tagout, Machine Guarding, Ergonomics, etc.)
10Optional Topics – 7 Hours

Assessment

  • →Written exam – Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  • →Minimum passing score: 70%

Career Opportunities

  • →Safety Supervisor
  • →Safety Officer
  • →Safety Consultant
  • →Safety Committee Member
