# Content Needed from Client

To upgrade the Highline Fire & Safety Training Institute website to a finished corporate standard, we need authentic, verifiable content to replace the template placeholders. Please supply the following:

## 1. Authentic Photography & Media
*   **Hero Section**: A high-quality photo or short video loop of actual trainers, classrooms, or practical drills at the Highline campus.
*   **Facilities**: Authentic photos of the Karimnagar campus, classrooms, smoke chamber, drill yard, and equipment.
*   **Trainers / Leadership**: Professional headshots and profiles (name, credentials, role) for the instructors.

## 2. Verified Statistics & Trust Metrics
*   Accurate numbers for the hero section and about page (e.g., Number of graduates? Years established? Number of active courses?). Do not invent numbers.

## 3. Placements & Corporate Ties
*   If placement assistance is provided, supply exact details on how it works.
*   Logos of companies where alumni have been placed (must have permission to use).
*   If campus placement drives happen, provide verifiable details.

## 4. Consented Testimonials
*   Real quotes from actual graduates, including their full name, the course they completed, and a photo (if they consent). We have removed the invented testimonials from the template.

## 5. Course Fees & Batch Dates
*   **Fees**: An approved list of fees for each course. If fees should not be public, we will use a "Contact for current fee" button.
*   **Batches**: Upcoming batch dates for the courses to populate the `batches.json` file.

## 6. Exact Accreditation Wording & Logos
*   Confirm if the following is the exact wording to be used globally: "EOSH Approved Training Provider · IOSH Approved Training Provider 5380 · Bharat Sevak Samaj (TEL/9473)".
*   Provide high-resolution logos for these accrediting bodies and confirm that Highline is authorized to display them on the website.

## 7. Legal Text
*   Drafts for `Privacy Policy` and `Terms & Conditions`.

## 8. Forms
*   Confirm the exact fields required for the Enquiry form and the Admissions form (e.g., Name, Phone, Email, Course of Interest, Message). Note: Aadhaar field will not be collected per constraints.
