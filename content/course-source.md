### 6.2 Qualification framework
Foundation (essential knowledge, core principles, workplace best practices) → Intermediate (practical skills, deeper understanding of workplace operations, risk management) → Advanced (technical expertise, problem-solving, compliance knowledge) → Professional (strategic leadership, governance, management) → Trainer (instructional, assessment and learner-engagement techniques; highest stage of the pathway). Learners "are encouraged to progress from Foundation and Intermediate qualifications through Advanced, Diploma, and Train the Trainer programmes".
