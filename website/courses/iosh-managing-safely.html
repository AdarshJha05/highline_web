Course Overview

IOSH is a globally recognised professional body that focuses on improving health and safety standards in workplaces. IOSH courses are designed to build safety awareness and promote safe behaviour at work. We offer IOSH-based training to help you understand how everyday safety practices can prevent accidents and injuries. Through this course, we explain the importance of identifying hazards, assessing risks, and following safe work practices in a simple and practical way. Our IOSH training is ideal for students, supervisors, and working professionals who want to improve safety awareness and contribute to a safer working environment.

Who Is It For?

Ideal for supervisors, team leaders, managers and beginners. 12th pass or above.

Benefits to Learners

  • →Build practical safety awareness to prevent workplace accidents
  • →Identify hazards and apply effective risk control measures
  • →Understand management responsibilities for workplace safety
